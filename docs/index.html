PGPO: Prototype-Guided Progressive Obfuscation for Privacy-Preserving LLM-Enhanced Recommendation

Abstract

Protect what an embedding reveals.
Preserve what recommendation needs.

LLM-enhanced recommender systems encode item side information into semantic embeddings, but these embeddings may leak sensitive textual attributes through inversion attacks. Existing input-side defenses rely on trusted collection or local token perturbation, often disrupting the inter-item semantic topology needed for recommendation. To address it, we introduce relational fidelity as the key objective, requiring semantic shift for privacy while preserving relative item relations for utility. Based on this principle, we propose prototype-guided progressive obfuscation (PGPO), which learns topology-aware obfuscation anchors for high-influence prototype words and propagates them through a static semantic graph. PGPO is optimized with GRPO using individual dissimilarity, structural consistency, and semantic constraint rewards. Experiments on two benchmarks show strong inversion defense with near-upper-bound recommendation performance.

Motivation

Semantic privacy is a relational problem.

Changing individual words is not enough. Useful recommendation signals live in the geometry between items; privacy protection must shift exposed content without dismantling that geometry.

01

Shift individual semantics

Move each protected representation away from its original textual source to make reconstruction difficult.

02

Preserve local structure

Maintain neighborhood ordering and relative relations so the recommender retains useful semantic evidence.

03

Optimize both together

Balance privacy and utility explicitly rather than treating perturbation as an isolated preprocessing trick.

Method

Prototype-guided progressive obfuscation.

PGPO first concentrates learning on structurally influential prototype words, then propagates the learned anchors through a static vocabulary-level semantic graph.

Stage 1

Construct a semantic graph

Build a static vocabulary graph and select high-influence prototype words that capture its structural backbone.

Stage 2

Learn prototype anchors

Apply Group Relative Policy Optimization to explore privacy-preserving variants for the selected prototypes.

Stage 3

Expand progressively

Generate variants for non-prototype words while conditioning on finalized neighbors and inherited topology.

Optimization objective

Three complementary rewards

Individual dissimilarity
Encourages semantic separation between each original word and its protected variant.
Structural consistency
Preserves the relational topology between neighboring words after obfuscation.
Semantic constraint
Controls the shift so variants remain useful and optimization stays stable.

Results

Strong privacy, near-upper-bound utility.

Across MovieLens-1M and Amazon-Book, PGPO substantially suppresses inversion while preserving recommendation performance close to using the original item descriptions.
